FAQs About Blood Tests & Health Checkups

Answers to common questions about booking, reports and home collection.

Where is your centre located?

We’re at 71 Valluvar Nagar, Annadhanapatty, Salem, Tamil Nadu 636002.

Is home collection available in my area?

Home collection may be available in areas including Fairlands, Hasthampatti, Shevapet, Suramangalam, Alagapuram and more. WhatsApp us your locality or PIN code to confirm.

How do I enquire about a test or package?

Call or WhatsApp +91 84385 81387 with the test or package name. We’ll confirm the current price, availability and any preparation instructions.

Is my booking confirmed when I send a WhatsApp message?

Not automatically. Your appointment or collection time is confirmed only after our team replies and confirms the details.

When will my report be ready?

Report time depends on the test. Ask our team for the estimated turnaround time when you book.

Can this website provide medical advice?

This website helps with test enquiries and booking; it cannot diagnose conditions or handle emergencies. Please discuss test choices and results with a qualified healthcare professional.
